# Luqmah Brand Styling

Use this skill whenever you are creating, redesigning, polishing, or reviewing Luqmah product surfaces, product copy, or branded artifacts.

**Keywords**: luqmah brand, luqmah design system, campus utility, warm product design, luqmah styling, luqmah colors, luqmah typography, luqmah theme

## Overview

Luqmah is a campus-first food ordering product. Its visual language should feel practical, warm, calm, and dependable. The brand should support fast task completion while still feeling welcoming and recognizably food-adjacent.

The same brand language applies across customer and vendor surfaces.

## Brand Principles

- Utility first
- Warmth without noise
- Calm confidence
- Same brand across roles
- Campus familiarity

## Color System

### Core Colors

- Primary Deep Green: `#15343A`
- Secondary Deep Green: `#214D4A`
- Warm Coral Accent: `#FF6B57`
- App Background: `#F6F7F9`
- Primary Surface: `#FFFFFF`
- Secondary Surface: `#EFF3F3`
- Strong Text / Shell Surface: `#122429`
- Muted Text: `#5F6B70`
- Borders: `#D8E0E1`

### Semantic Colors

- Success: `#1D8F55`
- Warning: `#C97A10`
- Info: `#0C87A8`
- Error: `#C2483C`

## Typography

- Direction: modern sans
- Prefer strong hierarchy with concise headings
- Keep body text practical and highly readable

### Scale Guidance

- Hero: 28-32
- Page title: 22-28
- Section title: 18-20
- Body: 14-16
- Helper: 12-13
- Chip and badge text: 11-12

## Shape And Layout

- Use rounded geometry
- Standard radii should generally fall between 12 and 24
- Use soft shadows and light borders
- Prefer comfortable compact density
- Maintain clean spacing and strong information hierarchy

## Components

- Buttons should be clean and confident
- Cards should feel light, soft, and structured
- Inputs should use filled neutral surfaces and clear labels
- Navigation should feel stable and persistent
- Chips and segmented controls should be subtle and legible

## Motion

- Motion should be subtle and smooth
- Avoid decorative animation
- Preserve stability during tab and page transitions

## Imagery

- Use structured and cropped food imagery
- Photos should support utility, not overwhelm it
- Provide graceful placeholders and error states

## Voice And Copy

- Warm and concise
- Supportive, not chatty
- Never over-market the product inside core task flows

## Accessibility

- Meet WCAG AA minimum expectations
- Preserve strong contrast and readable hierarchy
- Do not rely only on color to communicate important state

## Usage Rules

- Use this skill as the source of truth for Luqmah-themed artifacts
- Prefer centralized tokens and semantic roles over one-off colors
- Apply the same design language to both customer and vendor views
- Avoid separate ad hoc palettes for individual screens or roles

## Anti-Patterns

- Generic delivery app styling
- Cold enterprise dashboard styling
- Multiple unrelated green palettes
- Hardcoded one-off brand colors per screen
- Decorative visuals that reduce clarity
